How to Play

▼ Drop: Tap a column to drop the current number into it.

✚ Sum to 10: When adjacent numbers (up/down/left/right) form a connected group that sums to exactly 10, they clear!

▬ Full rows: A completely filled row clears too, just like Tetris.

⚡ Chains: After a clear, blocks fall. If new groups of 10 form, they chain-clear for bonus points!

⬆ Levels: Every 500 points, the level increases and the drop timer speeds up.

⚠ Game Over: If any column fills past the top, the game ends.
