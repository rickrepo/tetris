How to Play

Tap a column to drop the current number.

When 3 or more of the same number are connected (up/down/left/right), they all clear!

Blocks above fall down after a clear — chain reactions earn bonus points.

Full rows also clear, just like Tetris.

Game ends when any column reaches the top.
