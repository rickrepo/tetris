How to Play

Tap a column to drop the current number.

If two touching numbers add up to 10, they both clear.

Examples: 1+9, 2+8, 3+7, 4+6, 5+5

Full rows also clear, just like Tetris.

Cascading clears = chain bonus points!

Game over when a column overflows the top.
